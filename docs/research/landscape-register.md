# Landscape register

Primary-source comparison of execution and orchestration mechanisms relevant to
UH. Each row records a public source URL, version or commit where available,
retrieval date, quotation and adoption rationale. The machine-readable
`coordinator_check` field records the source-quotation check, not product
acceptance or a guarantee of current upstream behavior. Re-verify monthly or
when the row's `recheck_trigger` applies.

Rows: 118. Source check: 102 verified, 16 unverified.
